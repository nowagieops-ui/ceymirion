The question

What would distinguish the proposed effect from prompting, memory carryover or stylistic mimicry?

This paper sets out an empirical programme for challenging R.A.Y. It describes measurement classes, controls and experimental structures intended to test whether structured interaction produces behavioural recurrence beyond alternative explanations.

Research focus

  • Sets out control conditions and measurement classes.
  • Makes room for results that could weaken or reject the proposed explanation.
