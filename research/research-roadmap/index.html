The question

How do the public papers connect into a coherent research programme?

The roadmap brings the five preceding R.A.Y. papers into a single programme. It explains their relationship and the progression from conceptual framing and empirical challenge to governance, institutional engagement and responsible engineering.

Research focus

  • Connects the field, theory, empirical, strategic and ethics papers.
  • Provides a programme overview for research and institutional collaboration.
