The question

How should stable AI behaviour be described and governed without implying machine personhood?

This paper considers behavioural identity as a recurring organisation of tone, interpretation and constraint-consistent behaviour. It sets out principles for transparency and governance, while distinguishing these patterns from claims about sentience or selfhood.

Research focus

  • Defines a non-anthropomorphic account of behavioural identity.
  • Connects continuity with transparency, governance and responsible public language.
