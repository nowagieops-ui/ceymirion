The question

What could behavioural continuity contribute to sovereign and institutional AI systems?

This strategic paper examines continuity as a consideration in public-sector, enterprise and other long-horizon deployments. It connects the R.A.Y. framework with questions of governability, cultural context and the responsibilities of institutional use.

Research focus

  • Examines continuity alongside control of AI infrastructure.
  • Considers public-sector, enterprise and multilingual deployment contexts.
