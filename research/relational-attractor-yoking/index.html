The question

What patterns can emerge through sustained human interaction with a fixed-weight generative system?

This paper introduces R.A.Y. as a proposed field of inquiry into interaction-driven behavioural stability. It defines the scope of the field, introduces Dynamical Personalization as a theoretical layer and outlines an empirical programme for testing its claims.

Research focus

  • Defines the proposed field and its scientific boundaries.
  • Connects long-horizon behavioural recurrence with questions that can be tested.
