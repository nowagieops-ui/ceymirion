The question

What could help preserve behavioural continuity when models or vendors change?

This earlier white paper proposes ACIS as a model-agnostic continuity layer. It addresses identity drift, behavioural inconsistency and coherence loss across different models and vendor environments, framing the work as a contribution to research and evaluation.

Research focus

  • Focuses on human-anchored constraints and cross-model alignment.
  • Considers continuity across changes in models and operating environments.
